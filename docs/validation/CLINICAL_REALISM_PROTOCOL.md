# Clinical Realism Validation Protocol

## Reviewer Roles
- **Clinical Reviewer**: Validates clinical realism of scenarios.
- **Simulation Engineer**: Ensures technical accuracy of vitals generation.
- **Product Manager**: Oversees validation process and sign-off.

## Scenario Acceptance Thresholds
- **Accuracy**: Vitals must align with clinical ranges (±5% deviation).
- **Consistency**: Deterministic outputs must match expected trajectories.
- **Relevance**: Scenarios must reflect real-world clinical conditions.

## Sample Size
- Minimum of 100 samples per scenario.

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- **Pass**: ≥95% of samples meet acceptance thresholds.
- **Fail**: <95% of samples meet acceptance thresholds.
